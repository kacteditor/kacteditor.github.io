Tamil Nadu makes some of India's best products. Knitwear from Tiruppur, towels from Karur, wet grinders from Coimbatore, spices from Erode, brassware from Kumbakonam. Yet many of these makers struggle to sell online. Their listings get buried, ads burn money, and festival seasons pass by without a sales spike.

Kactii works with micro and small businesses across Tamil Nadu to change that. We handle the full journey on Amazon, from getting buyers to see your product to making sure you actually profit from every sale. Here is everything we offer.

## Get More Buyers to See Your Product

**Blogs**
Regular articles that bring outside visitors to your Amazon listings and build trust in your brand.

**Instagram Posts and Reels**
Consistent posts and short videos that grow brand awareness and send followers straight to your products.

**Amazon Ads**
Paid ads on Amazon managed weekly, so your money goes to the keywords that actually bring sales.

**Festival Campaigns**
Planned sales pushes for Pongal, Diwali, Prime Day, and Amazon's Great Indian Festival, prepared weeks in advance.

**Deals and Coupons**
Lightning Deals, coupons, and Prime-exclusive discounts timed to boost visibility when buyers are most active.

**Influencer Videos**
Short, honest product videos by Tamil creators that convert better than polished brand ads.

## Make Your Listing Convince Buyers

**Listing Writing**
Titles, bullet points, and search keywords written to rank higher and answer buyer questions.

**A+ Content and Brand Store**
Richer product pages and a branded storefront that make your products look premium.

**Product Photos and Video**
Clean, professional images and videos that show your product's quality at a glance.

**Tamil-Language Listings**
Amazon.in can be browsed in Tamil, so we create Tamil versions of your listings to reach more local buyers.

**Combo Packs**
Smart bundles such as a towel set of four or a podi combo that raise the value of every order.

## Price Right and Know Your Profit

**Seasonal Dynamic Pricing**
Prices adjusted by season and demand, always within Amazon's fair pricing rules.

**Profit Per Product**
Your true margin on each item after Amazon fees, ad spend, returns, and shipping.

**Competitor Tracking**
Alerts when a rival changes price or a new competitor enters your category.

**New Product Ideas**
Research on what to launch next, based on what buyers search for and what the market is missing.

**Review and Return Analysis**
We read your customer complaints and returns to find product or packaging fixes.

## Keep Your Account Running Smoothly

**Account Setup**
Opening your seller account, adding GST details, and setting up Fulfilled by Amazon or Easy Ship.

**Stock Alerts**
Reminders before your bestsellers run out, so you never lose sales or ranking.

**Account Health**
Fixing suppressed listings and policy warnings before they hurt your business.

**FBA Reimbursement Claims**
Recovering money from Amazon for inventory it lost or damaged in its warehouses.

**Buyer Message Replies**
Timely, polite replies to customer messages in Tamil and English.

## Stay Compliant

**Brand Registry and Trademark**
Trademark filing and Amazon Brand Registry to protect your brand and unlock premium features.

**Packaging Labels**
Labels that meet Legal Metrology rules, plus FSSAI for food and BIS where your category requires it.

**Barcodes**
Official GS1 barcodes for every product you sell.

## Grow Beyond Amazon

**Other Marketplaces**
Listing your products on Flipkart, Meesho, and JioMart to reach more buyers.

**Shopify Store**
Your own website, so your brand is never fully dependent on one marketplace.

**WhatsApp Catalog and Broadcasts**
A WhatsApp shop and broadcast lists to bring back repeat buyers.

**Monthly Owner Report**
One clear page of your key numbers every month, written in Tamil.
